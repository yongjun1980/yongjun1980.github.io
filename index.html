Universal Smart TV Remote

App support · Ryan Wen

Control compatible Android TV and Google TV devices from your phone, over the same local network.

Preparing for Google Play release. Compatibility depends on your TV firmware and the app currently running on your TV.

Free edition

  • Core remote controls and keyboard
  • Graphite theme
  • One saved TV
  • Banner advertising when available

Pro · One-time upgrade

  • Remove banner advertisements
  • Unlock all available themes
  • Save unlimited TVs
  • Sleep timer

Pro is optional and is not a subscription. Google Play shows your local price before you confirm a purchase. Availability and advertising depend on the released version, region and applicable privacy choices.

Get help

In the app, Settings → Report a problem prepares a draft for you to review and send in your email app.
